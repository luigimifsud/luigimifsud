# Clinic website

apexdentalmalta.com, the website for Apex Dental, my father's two dental clinics in Malta.

**Status:** live. **Dates:** 17 Mar to 25 Nov 2026.

## What it is

The clinic's public website, in English and Italian. It is my earliest project in this portfolio.

## Why it exists

Patients look a clinic up before they book. The clinic needed a site that explains its treatments clearly, in English and Italian, and sends people on to book.

## What I built

- A Vite/React site, hosted on Vercel.
- 67 pages, 20 of them in Italian.
- 527 commits between 17 Mar and 25 Nov 2026.

## Results

From Google Search Console: 128 clicks from search in the 4 weeks to 12 Nov 2026.

The commits show the name Jonathan Mifsud because I committed under my father's git identity.
