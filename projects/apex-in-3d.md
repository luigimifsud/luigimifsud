# Apex in 3D

An offline pipeline that turns the clinic's 3D treatment films into Instagram reels, rendered frame by frame.

**Status:** working, 5 reels ready, none posted yet. **Dates:** 28 Oct to 20 Nov 2026.

## What it is

Apex Dental, my father's clinics, has 3D films that show treatments. This pipeline turns them into Instagram reels and renders every frame offline. No AI image or voice models are used in the reels.

## Why it exists

Patients understand a treatment better when they can see it. The clinic already had the 3D films. What it did not have was a steady way to turn them into short reels.

## What I built

- The renderer, which builds each reel frame by frame, offline.
- 5 reels so far.

## Results

5 reels are rendered and ready to post.

## Status

Working. Posting is the clinic's decision.
