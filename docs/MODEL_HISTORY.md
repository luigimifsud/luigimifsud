# Every model I've built

This page is the history of the models I have built, from 20 Apr 2026, when my research platform Baseline started, to 26 Nov 2026. For each model it says what it was for, what it did and what it was measured on, with the limit beside the number. It does not say how any model is built.

Lower bits per byte is better. Most results are one run or one seed, and nobody outside my projects has re-run any of them yet. "No result outward" means I have no checked result for that model that I can publish here.

## Mark One (July to September 2026)

Baseline began on 20 Apr 2026, and Mark One became its language-model line: models I trained from scratch at home, from under a million to 295 million parameters, scored in bits per byte against standard compressors and against Qwen code models. They beat standard compressors on short held-out windows, which shows what they learned, not a better general-purpose compressor, and they lost to Qwen. Mark One was paused in September.

## QMK-1, QMK-2 and BMK (September 2026)

In September I started the [QMK line](cards/qmk-and-amk.md): programs with no trained neural weights, built under strict rules. QMK-1 read text and scored word pairs above chance, its memory store lost to plain similarity search, and it was switched off on 29 Sep; QMK-2, a counting compressor, lost to 7-Zip PPMd. Beside them, BMK 2, another counting compressor of mine, beat PPMd by small margins on slices of one fixed text sample, at far more computing time.

## QMK-3, JMK and QMK World (late September to October 2026)

From late September I built QMK World, a simulated Earth for a learner to try physics in, and moved the QMK line to QMK-3. QMK World failed its own physics exam, and QMK-3's early readings did not survive a check against a trivial method. JMK and a later race line were short races against my BMK 2 compressor: JMK lost and was withdrawn, and the later line edged it by a margin that its own verdict said did not earn its time.

## QMK-4, QMK-5, AMK, LIGHTGAP and two English models (October to mid-November 2026)

In October QMK-3's code became QMK-4, a program meant to keep reading English, and QMK-5 was a planned rewrite of it in Rust and CUDA. From 25 Oct to 2 Nov I wrote LIGHTGAP, my own text compressor, measured on English Wikipedia; its numbers so far are development numbers. On 26 and 27 Oct I trained two English language models from scratch beside that work, then put them away so the QMK and AMK work could take priority. Over about seven weeks the QMK and AMK models built under strict rules never got past reading back what they had been fed, and on 17 Nov I changed the rules.
